## 0.5.0

First release of Notch Event Bar, a personal fork reworked from NotchBar by Periicles (MIT, see `LICENSE`).

- Menu bar: remaining time plus the next event and the time until it, an optional mini progress bar instead of the icon, optional limit on the words of the title.
- Breaks between events measured from a configurable start of the day.
- Panel under the notch: dividers on the bar, capsules for meetings nested in a longer event, a bottom row with elapsed, remaining and what comes next, a Join button two minutes before a meeting.
- Settings: capsule colours, opacity and thickness, emoji labels, Refresh Events inside settings, same in the menu bar and the notch.
- Updates are checked against this repository only.
